Skills

Fourteen skills in three groups. Each has a required SKILL.md and may carry references/ and scripts/ for progressive detail — routing and execution stay in the front, deep detail moves out of the way.

Workflow

Eight skills cover the lifecycle from a rough idea to a persisted handoff. They lean on zharness to make state replayable and inspectable.

SkillPurpose
brainstormExplore options and lock requirements into an active plan.
to-planTurn a locked plan into phases, waves, tasks, and checks.
workExecute approved work and verify each task.
checkRun gates and review changes before shipping.
handoffPersist current state and the exact next action.
watzupReconstruct branch, lifecycle, and handoff state.
gitStage, commit, push, and prepare pull requests.
interviewResolve ambiguous intent through structured questions.

Craft

Four skills for the writing and research work that happens around the code.

SkillPurpose
writeWrite and edit concise English or Vietnamese prose.
librarianResearch code and evidence in external GitHub repositories.
create-skillCreate or improve Claude skills with references and validation.
prompt-leverageTurn rough prompts into execution-ready instructions.

Shipping

Two skills for building the things the workflow eventually ships.

SkillPurpose
create-cliDesign CLI commands, flags, I/O, errors, and delivery strategy.
turbo-mono-platformWork across the Turborepo, Next.js, Hono, tRPC, Drizzle, and Postgres stack.

Authoring a skill

Keep SKILL.md focused on routing and execution. Move deep detail into references/ and reusable logic into scripts/. Iterate locally with claude-code add-dir /path/to/mono-harness, then validate with bash scripts/validate-skill.sh before shipping. The full loop is under Contribute.
